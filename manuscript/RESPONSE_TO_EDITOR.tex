\textbf{2. Clarification: research vs review/perspective}

\emph{Kindly clarify whether this manuscript is a research or a review/perspective manuscript.}

This is a \textbf{Research} article; it is not a review or perspective. It reports an original, pre-registered empirical study: we generated a synthetic 600-case benchmark and collected 12,000 model screening decisions across five foundation models from two providers, and we report new quantitative results with inferential statistics (Cochran's $Q$, McNemar's exact test with Bonferroni correction, and Wilson and cluster-bootstrap confidence intervals), together with non-LLM baselines. Accordingly, we provide the Research-article Data Availability Statement under point~3.
